    Classical and Quantum Information

    • Entanglement Distribution in Quantum Switch Networks: Memory Limits and Achievability.
      L. Hu, A. Aytekin, S. Mitrolaris, M. Nomeir, S. Banerjee, S. Ulukus, and S. Guha.
      arXiv preprint, October 2026. [paper]
      Note: We characterize the minimum persistent switch memory for graph-state distribution and develop a polynomial-time online protocol that achieves this limit.
    • Simultaneously Minimizing Storage and Bandwidth Under Exact Repair With Quantum Entanglement.
      L. Hu, M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, and S. Ulukus.
      IEEE Information Theory Workshop (ITW), Tempe, Arizona, November 2026. [paper]
      Note: We explicitly construct an exact-repair scheme for d ≥ 2k − 2 that simultaneously minimizes storage and repair bandwidth.
    • Distributed Storage Systems with Quantum Entanglement Assistance.
      L. Hu, M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, and S. Ulukus.
      IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, to appear, 2026. [paper]
      Note: We give converse and achievability proofs for the fundamental tradeoff between storage and quantum repair bandwidth.
    • Breaking the Storage-Bandwidth Tradeoff in Distributed Storage with Quantum Entanglement.
      L. Hu, M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, and S. Ulukus.
      IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT), Guangzhou, China, June 2026. [paper] [slides]
      Note: We characterize the fundamental tradeoff between storage and quantum communication cost. Remarkably, for d ≥ 2k − 2, there exists a single operating point that simultaneously minimizes both. Such a point does not exist in classical systems.
    • Convergence Properties of Good Quantum Codes for Classical Communication.
      A. Aytekin, M. Nomeir, L. Hu, and S. Ulukus.
      IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT), Guangzhou, China, June 2026. [paper]
    • On the Capacity Region of Individual Key Rates in Vector Linear Secure Aggregation.
      L. Hu and S. Ulukus.
      IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT), Guangzhou, China, June 2026. [paper] [slides]
      Note: We provide new insights into the open individual-key-rate problem in Ref. [7] by deriving a new achievable region.
    • Quantum Private Distributed Matrix Multiplication: New Families of Quantum Codes.
      M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, L. Hu, and S. Ulukus.
      Submitted, August 2026. [paper]
    • Quantum Private Distributed Matrix Multiplication: Extending the Classical Codes and Limitations.
      M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, L. Hu, and S. Ulukus.
      Submitted, August 2026. [paper]
    • Quantum GASP Codes for Private Distributed Matrix Multiplication.
      M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, L. Hu, and S. Ulukus.
      IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing (ICASSP), Barcelona, Spain, May 2026. [paper]
    • Entanglement-Assisted Coding for Arbitrary Linear Computations Over a Quantum MAC.
      L. Hu, M. Nomeir, A. Aytekin, Y. Shi, S. Ulukus, and S. Guha.
      IEEE Information Theory Workshop (ITW), Sydney, Australia, September–October 2025. [paper] [slides]
      Note: We develop a new achievability scheme for linear computation over a quantum multiple-access channel (MAC). This gives a general scheme for the open problem in Ref. [14].
